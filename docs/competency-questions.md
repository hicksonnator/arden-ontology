# Arden Ontology Competency Questions v1.2.0

## Agriculture-Finance Bridge Competency Questions

### Primary Use Cases (Drive Term Selection):

1. **Loan Structuring**
   - "What repayment schedules are possible for a 5-year corn loan under ACRE tax treatment?"
   - "What is the amortization structure for a farmland mortgage tied to yield?"

2. **Risk Assessment**
   - "Which crops qualify for insurance securitization?"
   - "How do wheat futures prices affect collateral valuation for farm loans?"

3. **Compliance & Eligibility**
   - "Does this loan contract align with Farm Credit or Farmer Mac eligibility?"
   - "What tax implications apply to conservation easement financing?"

4. **Quantitative Conversion**
   - "Convert 150 bushels/acre to metric tonnes/hectare"
   - "Calculate loan-to-value ratio for 640-acre corn farm at $8000/acre"

5. **Market Integration**
   - "How do commodity futures affect agricultural loan pricing?"
   - "What's the collateral value of stored grain inventory?"

## Term Selection Criteria

**INCLUDE if supports competency questions above**
**EXCLUDE if purely academic or unused in ag-finance**
